About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Mathematics at South China University of Technology, advised by Professor Liming Ling.

Research interests

  • Integrable systems and nonlinear dispersive equations
  • Orbital stability of solitons and breathers
  • Rogue-wave patterns
  • Spectral and variational methods for nonlinear waves
